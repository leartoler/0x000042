Our language shapes us; it is what gives us substance. We are beings who are constantly becoming ourselves through language, moving from the most trivial, small, and ordinary things to that which transcends us. For within language lies our very being, and when it is revealed, we see a world surrounded by beauty, pain, acceptance, and solitude. In this unveiled beauty, we truly see the importance of the ordinary and realize, out of the corner of our eye, the insignificance of it all and our place within it.
